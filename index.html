Privacy Policy – My Work Hours

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Developer: Amateur Dev Studio

Contact: amateurdevstudio@gmail.com

1. Information stored by the app

My Work Hours allows users to record working dates, working hours, hourly rates, and calculated earnings. These records and app preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

2. Data collection and sharing

The developer does not operate a server that receives users' work records. The app does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising services, or account registration.

3. Exports and backups

Users may export reports and create backup files. Exported files and manual JSON backups are not separately encrypted by the app. When users choose to share or save these files using other apps or cloud services, those services may process the files under their own privacy policies.

4. Android system backups

Depending on Android settings and device configuration, the operating system may back up and restore app data through the user's configured backup service.

5. Data retention and deletion

Work records and preferences remain in local app storage until modified or deleted by the user or removed through device storage controls. Removing a record from the app does not automatically delete previously exported files or system backups.

6. Security

The app uses local device storage. Users should protect their devices and any exported files or backups. The developer cannot guarantee the security of external storage or third-party sharing services.

7. Children's privacy

My Work Hours is intended for general work-hour tracking and is not specifically directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact amateurdevstudio@gmail.com.
